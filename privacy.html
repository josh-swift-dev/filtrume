Privacy Policy

Filtrume — Last updated: September 30, 2026

Your photos stay yours. Filtrume is designed to edit photos directly on your device.

Overview

Filtrume respects your privacy. The app is designed to provide photo editing, filters, artistic effects, adjustments, cropping, and related features without requiring you to create an account.

Photos

Filtrume allows you to select photos from your device for editing. Photo processing is performed on your device.

Filtrume does not upload your photos to a Filtrume server or sell, share, or use your photos for advertising.

Photo Library Permission

Filtrume may request permission to add edited photos to your photo library when you choose to save an image.

This permission is used only to save the edited image you requested. Filtrume does not require unrestricted access to your entire photo library simply to save an edited image.

Personal Information

Filtrume does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, home address, or contacts.

Analytics and Tracking

Filtrume does not use your photos for advertising or cross-app tracking. Filtrume does not sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

Filtrume uses Apple-provided technologies and system frameworks to provide features such as photo selection, image processing, and saving edited images.

Your use of Apple services and your device is also subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Data Storage

Filtrume may store app preferences locally on your device, such as favorite filters and application settings. These preferences are used to provide app functionality.

Children's Privacy

Filtrume does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Filtrume changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
