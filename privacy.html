Privacy Policy

Filter Forge — Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Your privacy is important. Filter Forge is designed to provide photo editing and filtering tools while minimizing the collection of personal information.

Photos and Photo Library Access

Filter Forge may request permission to access photos you choose for editing and permission to save edited photos to your photo library.

Photos selected for editing are processed on your device. Filter Forge does not upload your photos to a developer-operated server or use your photos for advertising.

Photo Saving

When you choose to save an edited image, Filter Forge uses Apple's photo library services to save the resulting image to your device's photo library.

Personal Information

Filter Forge does not require you to create an account and does not require your name, address, phone number, or other personally identifying information to use its photo editing features.

Analytics and Tracking

Filter Forge does not use your photos for tracking or advertising and does not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Filter Forge relies on Apple technologies and system services to provide features such as photo selection, image processing, and saving photos. Use of Apple services is subject to Apple's applicable policies.

Children's Privacy

Filter Forge does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Filter Forge evolves. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
