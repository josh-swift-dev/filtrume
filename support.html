Filter Forge Support

Need help with Filter Forge? You're in the right place.

Getting Started

Open Filter Forge and select a photo to begin editing. Browse the available filter collections and tap a filter to preview it on your image.

Applying Filters

Filter Forge includes a large collection of photographic looks, color tints, film-inspired styles, cinematic looks, black-and-white filters, creative effects, and other presets.

Select a filter to apply it to your photograph. You can adjust the intensity of the selected look to control how strongly it affects your image.

Manual Adjustments

Use the Adjust section to fine-tune your photograph. Available controls may include exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation, vibrance, warmth, tint, highlights, shadows, sharpness, vignette, and glow.

Compare With the Original

Use the before-and-after comparison feature while editing to compare your current edit with the original photograph.

Crop and Rotate

The Crop section provides common aspect ratios and rotation controls for changing the composition and orientation of your image.

Saving a Photo

Tap Save when you are finished editing. Filter Forge will render the edited image and save the finished photo to your photo library.

If saving does not work, verify that Filter Forge has permission to add photos to your photo library in your device's privacy settings.

Original Photos

Filter Forge uses non-destructive editing. Editing a photograph in Filter Forge does not overwrite the original photo in your library. The finished edit is saved as a separate image.

Having Trouble?

If you experience an issue, restarting Filter Forge and confirming that photo permissions are enabled may resolve the problem.

When contacting support, including your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened can help diagnose the issue.
