Filtrume Support

Photo editing. Reimagined.

Need help with Filtrume? You're in the right place.

Getting Started

Open Filtrume and tap Choose a Photo to select an image.

You can then browse Filtrume's collection of photo looks, color effects, film-inspired styles, artistic effects, drawing effects, and other creative filters.

Adjusting a Filter

After selecting a look, use the adjustment controls to customize the image. Filtrume includes controls for features such as exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation, vibrance, warmth, tint, highlights, shadows, sharpness, vignette, and glow.

Comparing With the Original

While editing, use the compare feature to temporarily view the original photo and compare it with your edited version.

Crop and Rotation

The Crop section provides multiple aspect ratios and rotation controls so you can prepare your image for different uses.

Saving Photos

Tap Save when you are finished editing. Filtrume renders the edited image and saves the completed photo to your photo library.

iOS may ask Filtrume for permission to add photos to your library. This permission is necessary for Filtrume to save your finished image.

Can't Save a Photo?

If photo-saving permission was previously denied, open your iPhone Settings, locate Filtrume, and allow Filtrume permission to add photos. Then return to the app and try saving again.

Your Original Photo

Editing in Filtrume is non-destructive. Saving an edited photo creates an edited result rather than intentionally replacing your original photo.
