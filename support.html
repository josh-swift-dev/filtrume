Filtrume Support

Create your look. Mix your filters. Keep your original.

Getting Started

Filtrume is a non-destructive photo editor with more than 250 original looks, creative effects, manual adjustments, filter stacking, crop tools, and on-device editing features.

  1. Open Filtrume.
  2. Tap Choose a Photo.
  3. Select a photo from your Photos library.
  4. Choose a filter or effect.
  5. Fine-tune your image using the editing controls.
  6. Tap Save when you're finished.
Filtrume keeps your original photo untouched. Saving creates an edited copy rather than replacing your original.

Applying Filters

Browse the available filter categories and tap a filter thumbnail to apply it to your photo.

The active filter is visually marked so you can quickly see what is currently applied.

Tap an active filter again to remove it.

You can also use the Filter Intensity control to make the selected look stronger or more subtle.

Filter Stacking

Filtrume lets you decide whether you want a simple one-filter workflow or want to combine multiple filters.

Stack OFF

By default, you can work with one filter at a time. When Stack is turned off, selecting another filter replaces the currently selected filter.

This is useful when you simply want to browse different looks and choose your favorite.

Stack ON

Tap the Stack button in the filter controls to turn filter stacking on.

When stacking is enabled, the Stack control illuminates so you can immediately tell that Stack Mode is active.

You can then combine up to 5 filters on the same image.

Stack Mode is great for creating looks that don't exist as a single preset. For example, you can combine a color treatment, cinematic look, and creative effect into one edit.

Changing the Stack Order

Filters in a stack are applied in order. Because one effect can change the result of the next effect, changing their order can produce a different final image.

Move filters within the stack to experiment with the order in which the effects are applied.

Removing a Filter From a Stack

Remove an individual filter from the stack using its remove control. The remaining filters stay active.

Turning Stack Mode Off

Tap Stack again to turn stacking off.

If multiple filters are currently stacked, Filtrume returns to single-filter mode and keeps the most recently applied filter.

Comparing With the Original

Want to see how far you've taken your photo?

Press and hold the photo while editing to temporarily view the untouched original.

Release your finger to return to your edited version.

This is one of the fastest ways to judge whether an edit is improving the photo or becoming too strong.

Manual Adjustments

Open Adjust for additional control over your image.

Available adjustments include:

Exposure Brightness Contrast Saturation Vibrance Warmth Tint Highlights Shadows Sharpness Vignette Glow

Manual adjustments work alongside your selected filters, allowing you to customize a preset instead of being locked into its default appearance.

Crop, Rotate & Flip

Open the Crop section to change the framing of your photo.

You can:

  • Crop the image.
  • Choose supported aspect ratios.
  • Rotate the image.
  • Flip the image horizontally.
  • Reset the crop and orientation.

AI Tools

Filtrume includes supported on-device AI-assisted editing tools directly inside the editor.

Open the AI section to access available intelligent editing options and adjust their intensity when supported.

Availability and results can vary depending on the photo, subject, and device capabilities.

Favorites

With more than 250 looks available, Favorites gives you a faster way to return to the filters you use most.

Tap the heart on a filter to add or remove it from Favorites.

Open the Favorites category to quickly find your saved looks.

Undo

If you make a change you don't like, use the Undo control at the top of the editor.

Filtrume keeps recent editing states so you can safely experiment with filters, adjustments, crop settings, and other edits.

Saving Your Photo

When you're happy with your edit, tap Save.

Filtrume renders the finished image and saves a full-resolution copy to your Photos library.

Your original photo remains unchanged.

Filtrume Can't Save

Filtrume needs permission to add the finished image to your Photos library.

If permission was previously denied, open your iPhone's Settings and allow Filtrume to add photos.

Photo Privacy

Filtrume is designed around on-device photo editing. Your photos remain under your control.

Filtrume only requests the permissions needed for features such as selecting and saving photos.

Troubleshooting

A filter isn't changing the photo

Make sure the filter is active. If Stack Mode is off, selecting a different filter replaces the previous filter.

I can't select several filters

Tap Stack to enable filter stacking. When Stack Mode is off, Filtrume intentionally allows only one filter at a time.

I can't add another filter to my stack

A stack can contain up to 5 filters. Remove one of the active filters before adding another.

My photo looks different after rearranging the stack

That's expected. Filter order matters because each filter receives the result produced by the filters before it.

Saving doesn't work

Check that Filtrume has permission to add photos to your Photos library.

The preview takes a moment to update

Some creative effects, stacked filters, AI-assisted tools, and complex edits require more processing than a simple color adjustment. Processing time can vary by device and image.
